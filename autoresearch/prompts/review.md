Write the final report to:

autoresearch/runs/{task_id}/report.md

Independent validation has already passed. This pass records the outcome. Do not change product code, controller code, or evaluation definitions.

For a research experiment, separate these sections:

- Hypothesis
- Change made
- Pre-experiment prediction
- Experiment configuration
- Results
- Visual behavior
- Interpretation
- Evidence for/against hypothesis
- Confounders / uncertainty
- Decision
- Possible follow-up

For an engineering or refactoring task, write an engineering report instead. Include what changed and why, what was deliberately left alone, which checks ran, and what remains uncertain.

Do not claim checks succeeded unless they were executed. Do not hide failed approaches.
